# Spec: History, Favorites and Dashboards

## History
When implemented, generation history MAY record date/time, generator, environment, type, quantity, basic parameters and result/reference for reuse. Avoid unnecessary data and secrets.

## Favorites
Users SHALL be able to favorite generators and access them quickly from Sidebar/Dashboard.

## Dashboards
Each environment SHALL have its own dashboard. General MAY expose most-used/recent/favorite generators, counts and shortcuts. Port MAY expose recent port generators, scenarios, generated masses, favorites and shortcuts.
